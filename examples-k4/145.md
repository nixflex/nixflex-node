Server notes:
- The from date must precede the exclusive day after to and cannot be in the future. Dates must be real YYYY-MM-DD dates. CSV uses 500-row chunks and streams the full matching result; 500 is not a total export limit. Permissions are checked for every chunk. An interrupted download is incomplete. CSV columns are When, Action and Result. The candidate kits support before and actor only; use direct HTTP for date filters or CSV. The older Node ActivityEvent type does not match this displayed response; use the fields documented here.
